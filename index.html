Questions, answered.

The short version. The rest we cover on the call.

What exactly do I pay?

Once, the agent’s price: €149, €249 or €490 + VAT, shown on its page. A whole department costs 25% less than its agents one by one; all 16 available agents cost €2,490. Installation, tuning and 30 days of support included. After that, Support is optional: from €49/month, server included.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes, with a plan: from €79/month + VAT, with the agents you choose, AI credits, setup and our server included. Minimum 3 months, then cancel anytime. See the plans

Can I buy an agent online right now?

Not with one click. Every agent is set up for your business, so we start with a short call. You get a fixed price before anything begins.

Any other costs?

You pay us once, plus Support only if you want it. The agent runs on an AI service under an account in your name: you pay that usage straight to the provider, with no markup. On the call we tell you what it costs in your case.

What happens after 30 days?

The agent stays yours and keeps working: on your machine for €0/month, or on our server with Support from €49/month. No minimum term, cancel anytime.

Do I need anyone technical?

No. We install it and tune it. You read what the agent prepares and decide.

Can it send emails or post on its own?

Only if you decide so. By default it prepares everything and waits for your OK.

What does “Coming soon” mean?

We’re building that agent. Join the waitlist from its page and we’ll write when it’s ready.

Where does my data go?

It stays in your tools and accounts. We don’t use it for other clients and we don’t share it with anyone.
