Questions

Straight answers.

What do I need?

A Gmail or Google Workspace inbox. We look at other inboxes together.

Can it reply for me?

No. It drafts at most, and you read and send.

Who sees my data?

Your mail stays in your inbox. The agent reads it to sort it and write the summary, and forwards it to no one.

Can I use it for my clients?

Yes. The agent is yours: use it for your business and for the work you do for your clients.

Do I need to know about AI?

No. We install it and tune it. You read what it proposes and decide.

Any other costs?

You pay us once, plus Support only if you want it. The agent runs on an AI service under an account in your name: you pay that usage straight to the provider, with no markup. On the call we tell you what it costs in your case.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes, with a plan: from €79/month + VAT, with the agents you choose, AI credits, setup and our server included. Minimum 3 months, then cancel anytime.

What happens after 30 days?

The agent stays yours and keeps working: on your machine for €0/month, or on our server with Support from €49/month. No minimum term, cancel anytime.
